This is a project to train a deep neural network to detect whether a sentence is about a dog or not.

## Examples

**Detected as about a dog:**
- "My golden retriever loves chasing the ball at the park."
- "The puppy wouldn't stop barking all night."
- "She grabbed the leash and took him for a walk."
- "He's a very good boy who fetches the newspaper every morning."

**Detected as NOT about a dog:**
- "The cat curled up on the windowsill in the sun."
- "I need to buy groceries after work today."
- "The stock market fell sharply this afternoon."
- "Her favorite subject in school is chemistry."
